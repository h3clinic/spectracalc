How this works

Dots are rebuilt from the published 0.1 nm data pushed back through this page's frozen calibration, so they land on the printed ink exactly as the round-trip validation shows. Edit them and the charts and downloads update live.

Saving publishes. Anyone visiting the site will see your version of this spectrum — its charts, its peak wavelengths and its CSV download — until someone edits it again or reverts it. Every version is kept, and the original digitization can always be restored from the viewer.
